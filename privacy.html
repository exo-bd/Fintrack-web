Introduction

FinTrack ("FinTrack", "we", "our", "us") is a personal finance app for Android, developed under the package com.fintrack.app. This policy explains what information the app accesses, why, and how it is stored. It applies to anyone who installs or uses FinTrack.

FinTrack is built around a simple idea: your financial data should help only you. Wherever possible, information is processed on your device rather than sent anywhere.

We process personal data on the basis of your consent, the performance of our agreement with you, and our legitimate interest in providing the app's core features. FinTrack is operated from Bangladesh and used in Bangladesh and elsewhere, so this policy is written to apply globally, while also honoring specific rights granted under frameworks such as the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA), and Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Ordinance, 2025 — summarized in Regional disclosures.

By creating an account or enabling a feature that requires a permission (such as the camera or notifications), you consent to the related data use described in this policy. You can withdraw that consent at any time by disabling the permission or deleting your account — see Account & data deletion.

Information we collect

FinTrack collects only what it needs to run your ledger. We treat your financial data as sensitive information and apply additional care in how it is stored, accessed, and shared:

  • Account information — your name, email address, and profile photo, when you sign in with Google or create an account, via Firebase Authentication.
  • Multiple accounts — the names, types (such as cash, bank, or mobile wallet), and balances for each account you set up to track.
  • Financial data you create — transactions, budgets, categories, debts, recurring bills, to-do items, and notes, whether entered manually or scanned from a receipt.
  • Camera and photos — receipt images you choose to scan or photos you choose to attach, processed to extract amounts and dates.
  • Voice input — audio captured only while you actively use voice entry to add a transaction by speaking. It is converted to text by your device's speech-recognition service (provided by Google or your device maker, which may process audio online depending on your device settings). FinTrack does not store the audio.
  • Biometric data — handled entirely by your device's operating system to unlock the app. FinTrack never receives or stores your fingerprint or face data.
  • AI insight summary data — once a month, an aggregated summary of your activity (such as total income, total expenses, savings amount, and the percentage of spending in each category) is generated and stored, as described in How AI insights work below. This summary never includes your individual transactions, merchant names, account numbers, or notes.
  • Community activity — your display name and any posts, comments, or progress you choose to share are visible to other users inside the Community feature.
  • Gamification & reward data — points, streaks, levels, and reward progress generated from your in-app activity.
  • Device & diagnostic data — basic technical information (device model, OS version, crash logs via Firebase Crashlytics) used to keep the app stable, plus device-integrity signals used to detect tampered or unsafe environments.
  • Fraud-prevention record — when you register, we create a record keyed by a one-way (keyed-hash) fingerprint of your email address, with flags showing whether a promotional offer or free trial was used and your assigned FinTrack ID and group letter. It contains no plain-text email or name and is kept after account deletion — see Retention policy.
  • Purchase records — Google Play order and purchase-token identifiers for premium purchases (we never see your card details).

How we use your information

  • To create and maintain your accounts, transaction ledger, budgets, debts, recurring bills, and to-do items.
  • To sync your data securely across your own devices when you're signed in, and back it up to the cloud.
  • To generate your monthly AI insight by analyzing an aggregated summary of your activity — see How AI insights work for exactly what this involves.
  • To send budget alerts, bill reminders, and reward notifications you've enabled.
  • To power Community features, such as displaying your name and shared progress to other users.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, or technical issues.
  • To improve app stability and performance.
FinTrack does not use your financial data or receipt images to build an advertising profile, and does not sell this data to anyone.

How AI insights work

AI Insights turn your own entries into plain-language observations — for example, a category where spending is rising, or a stronger savings rate than usual. Here is exactly what happens:

  • 1. Calculated on your device. Insights are produced by FinTrack on your phone from data you have already entered (income, expenses, savings, category percentages and your past months). Your individual transactions, merchant names, account numbers and notes are not sent anywhere to generate them.
  • 2. No external AI provider. Your data is not sent to OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, or any other external AI service for this feature.
  • 3. Stays with your account. Like the rest of your app data, insights are covered by cloud backup when you are signed in, under your account and with the per-account isolation described in Storage & security.
  • 4. Not financial advice. Insights are a starting point for reflection. If we ever change how insights are produced, we will update this section before the change takes effect.

Your data, including insights, is retained for as long as your account is active — see Retention policy.

Storage & security

  • Local-first: your ledger is stored on your device first, in FinTrack's private app storage (protected by the Android app sandbox and, on supported devices, device encryption), before anything syncs to the cloud. You can add a PIN, pattern or biometric lock to the app.
  • Cloud sync: when signed in, data syncs to Cloud Firestore under your own Firebase account, governed by per-account access rules so one account can never read another's data.
  • Sensitive fields: sign-in tokens are kept in encrypted secure storage provided by the Android operating system; your app PIN or pattern is stored on the device only as a salted hash, never in plain text.
  • App integrity: FinTrack uses Firebase App Check (Google Play Integrity) and the freeRASP security library to detect tampered, rooted or emulated environments. This processes device-integrity signals and may restrict features on compromised devices.
  • Account isolation: on a shared device, switching accounts does not expose another account's transactions, points, or settings.

Account & data deletion

You can permanently delete your FinTrack account and associated data at any time:

  • In the app: go to Settings → Account → Delete Account, and confirm with the code we email you.
  • From the web, without the app: visit our account deletion page — useful if you've already uninstalled FinTrack.

Once verified, we permanently delete your account and the personal data tied to it — we do not just deactivate or freeze it. A small set of data is kept for fraud prevention and legal reasons; exactly what, and why, is listed in Retention policy.

Third-party services

FinTrack relies on a small set of providers to operate:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, App Check, Crashlytics) — sign-in, data sync, cloud backup, push notifications, abuse protection and crash reports.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in using your Google account.
  • Google Play Billing & Play Integrity — premium purchases (FinTrack never sees your card details) and device/app integrity checks.
  • Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform — ads on the free tier and the consent prompt shown where required.
  • Google ML Kit (text recognition) — reads text from receipt images you choose to scan, on your device.
  • freeRASP (Talsec) — app-security checks that detect tampering, rooting and similar threats.
  • Gmail (Google) — used to send you account sign-in, verification and deletion-confirmation emails.
  • Telegram — only if you choose to contact our support bot; Telegram processes that conversation under its own terms.

AI Insights do not use a third-party AI provider — see How AI insights work.

Each provider processes data under its own privacy terms in addition to this policy.

International data transfers

FinTrack is operated and developed in Bangladesh, and our service providers — particularly Google Firebase and Google Cloud — may store and process data in other countries, including the United States and other regions where Google operates infrastructure. If you are located outside the country where your data is processed, your information may be transferred internationally. We use the same data practices and protections regardless of where you are located.

Where required by applicable law (for example, the GDPR for EEA/UK residents), such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses or other mechanisms recognized as providing an adequate level of protection. By using FinTrack, you understand that your data may be processed outside your country of residence as described here.

Advertising

FinTrack shows ads through Google AdMob on free-tier screens. Depending on your region and consent choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. You can manage ad personalization in your device's Google Settings, and remove ads entirely with a premium subscription.

Data sharing & disclosure

We do not sell your personal or financial data. We share data only with the service providers listed above, as needed to operate the app, or when required by law.

Retention policy

Your data — including your ledger, budgets, history and insights — is retained for as long as your account is active, so that your full history and trends remain available to you.

If you delete your account (see Account & data deletion), your personal data is permanently deleted, except for this limited set, kept only for the stated purpose:

  • Fraud-prevention record — a keyed one-way hash of your email address, flags that a promotional offer or free trial was used, and your previous FinTrack ID and group letter. Purpose: stop repeated sign-ups with the same email from abusing promotions or free trials. It contains no plain-text email or name, the email cannot be recovered from it, and it is not shared or used for advertising.
  • Purchase records — Google Play order and purchase-token identifiers, kept for refund verification, accounting and legal obligations.
  • Security logs — logs needed to investigate fraud, abuse or security incidents, kept only as long as necessary.

Children's privacy

FinTrack requires users to be at least 18 years old (see our Terms & Conditions), which is above the digital consent age in every jurisdiction we're aware of, including the EU/UK's GDPR (typically 13–16) and the US's COPPA (13). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Your rights

Wherever you are located, and subject to applicable law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or outdated information, most of which you can edit directly in the app.
  • Delete your account and associated data at any time — see Account & data deletion.
  • Restrict or object to specific processing, such as turning off voice entry, AI insights, or ad personalization, while continuing to use the rest of the app.
  • Withdraw consent for any permission-based feature at any time from Android Settings, without losing access to your previously saved data.
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format by contacting us.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Contact us, or use our Support page. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law, or otherwise within a reasonable time.

Regional disclosures

Depending on where you live, you may have additional or more specific rights under local law. The sections below summarize how some of the most common frameworks apply to FinTrack; they supplement, and do not replace, the general rights described above. If your country isn't listed, contact us — we'll honor requests consistent with the privacy rights available to you locally wherever we reasonably can.

European Economic Area & United Kingdom (GDPR / UK GDPR)

  • Our legal basis for processing is, depending on the feature: your consent (for permission-gated features like the camera or voice entry), the performance of our contract with you (for core ledger and sync features), and our legitimate interest (for security, fraud prevention, and service improvement).
  • You have the rights to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and to object to processing, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out.
  • You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

California, USA (CCPA / CPRA)

  • The categories of personal information we collect are described in Information we collect above — including identifiers, financial information, commercial information, and audio/visual information you provide (such as voice input or receipt photos).
  • We do not sell personal information for money. Displaying personalized ads through Google AdMob may be considered "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising under the CCPA/CPRA; you can opt out by turning off ad personalization in your device's Google Settings, or by contacting us to opt out of personalized ads entirely.
  • You have the right to know, delete, and correct your personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights.
  • An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with proof of authorization.

Bangladesh (Personal Data Protection Ordinance, 2025)

Bangladesh's Personal Data Protection Ordinance, 2025 recognizes you as the rightful owner of your personal data, requires explicit consent before collection or use, and classifies financial information as sensitive data warranting enhanced protection — consistent with the practices described throughout this policy.

Other countries

If your country provides additional statutory privacy rights not explicitly listed here — for example, under Canada's PIPEDA, Brazil's LGPD, India's DPDP Act, or Australia's Privacy Act — we will honor requests consistent with those rights to the extent required by applicable law. Contact us using the details below to exercise any right available to you locally.

Any dispute arising from this policy will be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Bangladesh. This does not limit any mandatory consumer-protection or data-protection rights you have under the law of your own country of residence — for example, under the GDPR for EEA/UK residents or the CCPA for California residents — which continue to apply to the extent applicable law requires.

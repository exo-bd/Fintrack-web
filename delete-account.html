Uninstalling the app does not delete your account

Your account and data stay on our servers until you delete them using one of the options below.

1) In the app (fastest)

Open FinTrack → Settings → Account → Delete Account. We email a confirmation code to your account email; enter it to confirm and your account and data are deleted.

2) Without the app

If the app is uninstalled or you can't sign in, email us from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my FinTrack account". Including your FinTrack ID (shown on your profile, like fintrack_A0000001) helps us find it faster.

Email deletion request Telegram support

After verifying the request we normally finish within 7 business days, and no later than 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and sign-in account (Firebase Authentication)
  • Transactions, budgets, accounts, debts and loans, recurring bills, reminders and to-dos
  • Cloud backups and synced receipt images
  • Points, streaks and reward progress, your referral code, and community submissions

What is kept, and why

DataPurpose
A one-way (keyed-hash) fingerprint of your email, flags that a promotional offer or free trial was used, and your previous FinTrack ID and group letter Fraud prevention — stops repeated sign-ups with the same email from abusing promotions or free trials. It holds no plain-text email or name, and the email cannot be recovered from it.
Google Play purchase records (order / token IDs)Refund verification, accounting and legal obligations.
Security logsInvestigating fraud, abuse or security incidents, kept only as long as necessary.

This data is never sold, never shared for advertising, and is described in our Privacy Policy.
